The most interesting content in the world is written. Now, you can listen to all of it, clip, share, and more — with the brand-new TTSReader app for iPhone.

## Exciting Launch of TTSReader for iPhone!! The BEST Text-to-Speech App for iPhone ever built!

<br/>

Open the app and land on Discover — founding documents, famous speeches, classic books and stories, curated and ready to play.

![The Discover tab with The Declaration of Independence as the featured item.](01-discover.jpg)

Your Library holds everything in one place — documents, ebooks, and rendered audio, with quick filters to find any of them.

![The Library tab with add shortcuts and recent documents, books, and audio.](02-library.jpg)

And the best part? It plays on CarPlay!!! And supports 100% OFFLINE playing!!

![TTSReader on CarPlay](carplay.jpg)


Adding something new takes one tap: a blank page, your clipboard, a web page, a file — or let AI teach you about any topic or recap today's news.

![The New Document sheet: blank page, clipboard, web page, file import, AI lesson, and news recap.](03-new-document.jpg)

Pick from clear, pleasant, natural-sounding voices — favorite the ones you love, and preview any of them with a tap.

![The Voices tab with favorite multilingual voices and previews.](04-voices.jpg)

The reader is built for long, comfortable sessions — here's Moby Dick in the warm sepia theme, chapter by chapter.

![Moby Dick chapter one in the sepia reading theme with the player below.](05-reader-sepia.jpg)

As it reads, the current sentence is highlighted so your eyes and ears stay in sync — even at night.

![The Constitution playing in the black theme with the current sentence highlighted.](06-highlight-night.jpg)

Default, Paper, Sepia, Night, or Black — the whole reading experience adapts to the theme you choose.

![Two phones showing the night and sepia themes with the theme picker.](07-themes.jpg)

Text size, font, colors — tune the page until it reads exactly the way you like.

![The Appearance sheet with text size, font choices, and reading themes.](08-appearance.jpg)

Select any passage to copy, translate, speak — or turn it straight into an audio clip.

![A selected passage with the Generate audio clip button and text actions.](09-select-to-clip.jpg)

Your clip is rendered in seconds with the voice of your choice — save it to Files or share it anywhere.

![The Audio clip sheet with play preview, Save to Files, and Share.](10-audio-clip.jpg)

Render whole documents to audio in the cloud — download them for offline listening, or add them to your personal podcast channel.

![The Render audio sheet with voice, character count, podcast, and offline options.](11-render-audio.jpg)

Rendered audio is yours to keep: save the MP3 to your device folders or share it with anyone.

![The rendered audio menu: save MP3 to device folders, share, remove download.](12-mp3-share.jpg)

Built-in AI tools go further — summarize a document, translate it, or turn it into a two-host podcast.

![The document menu with AI actions: Summarize, Translate, and Podcast.](13-ai-tools.jpg)

Reading something interesting in your browser? TTSReader is one share away.

![A Wikipedia article in the browser with the share menu open.](14-wikipedia.jpg)

Share any page straight into TTSReader — it lands in your Library and starts reading aloud.

![The iOS share sheet with TTSReader among the share targets.](15-share-sheet.jpg)

Ebooks in your Files app? TTSReader picks them up too.

![The Files app showing EPUB files with the TTSReader icon.](16-files-epub.jpg)

Open any EPUB with TTSReader and it becomes a narrated book, chapters and all.

![Opening an EPUB from Files directly in TTSReader.](17-open-with.jpg)

And for the perfectionists: a pronunciation dictionary lets you teach your voices exactly how to say any word.

![The pronunciation dictionary with a custom rule and a test button.](18-pronunciation.jpg)

That's the new TTSReader for iPhone — the content you always meant to read, finally accessible right on your phone - anywhere you go.
